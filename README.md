<h2>🌎 Konsènan nou / About Us</h2>

<table>
<tr>
<th align="left">Konsènan nou</th>
<th align="left">About Us</th>
</tr>

<tr>
<td valign="top">

AyitiDev se yon kominote open source ki rasanble devlopè, etidyan, pwofesyonèl, ak moun ki renmen teknoloji pou kolabore sou pwojè ki gen enpak.

Nou konekte moun ann Ayiti ak dyaspora ayisyèn pou pataje konesans, aprann ansanm, epi konstwi solisyon teknoloji pou lavni.

</td>

<td valign="top">

AyitiDev is an open-source community bringing together developers, students, professionals, and technology enthusiasts to collaborate on impactful projects.

We connect people in Haiti and the Haitian diaspora to share knowledge, learn together, and build technology solutions for the future.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

---

<h2>🎯 Objektif / Goals</h2>

<table>
<tr>
<th align="left">Objektif</th>
<th align="left">Goals</th>
</tr>

<tr>
<td valign="top">

Objektif nou se kreye yon ekosistèm teknoloji kote moun ka kolabore sou pwojè open source, devlope konpetans yo, epi pote lide yo bay lavi.

Nou vle fè teknoloji pi aksesib epi kreye plis opòtinite pou kominote ayisyen an.

</td>

<td valign="top">

Our goal is to create a technology ecosystem where people can collaborate on open-source projects, develop their skills, and bring their ideas to life.

We want to make technology more accessible and create more opportunities for the Haitian community.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
